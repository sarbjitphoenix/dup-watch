Multi-Subscription Launch

Deployed to production 18 Aug 2026 · adoption as of 25 Aug 2026, 02:41 UTC

Customers can now hold more than one subscription at the same time. The feature is enabled for all clients and all stores at the store level (one subscription per store, so a customer can subscribe in several stores of a client's network) and for four clients at the billing-type level (multiple subscriptions inside the same store across different billing types); additional clients will be enabled at the billing-type level once the list is finalized. Creation, cancellation, and the scope settings have been monitored in production since launch and are behaving as expected.
